    Method

    Five lenses, scored 1 to 5.

    LensQuestion135

    Evidence

    • Served HTML of the homepage and the Founder Primer, then the live pages in a desktop browser, both on 27 November 2026.
    • Header nav, footer, /sitemap.xml (2 URLs) and /robots.txt.
    • Probes of /about and /privacy, which returned errors.
    • Stop score = sum of five lenses ÷ 25 × 100. Site score = mean of stop scores.

    Limits

    • A one-page site, so homepage sections are scored as stops. Findable is read per section: nav link, heading, served content.
    • Screenshots come from one desktop browser width, 800px. Mobile layouts weren't checked.
    • No traffic, speed or accessibility tooling data. The booking calendar wasn't scored.
    • Scores are editorial judgement against the lens anchors.
