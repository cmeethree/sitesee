The product pages do their job

BrazeAI, Data Platform, Cross-Channel, Email and Decisioning Studio share one clear template with named customers, analyst proof and FAQs. Decisioning Studio is the best page on the site.

The conversion path is the weak link

Six differently worded CTAs all land on /get-started, whose served HTML has about 45 words and no visible form. Two pages quietly use better inline forms.

Trust erodes in the details

Brand counts range from 1,700+ to 2,700+ across pages, test pages are indexable, duplicate URLs compete, and unfinished sections ship to production.

Verdict

Does the site work?

Maybe59 / 100

Strong message.
Weak hygiene.

The site score is 59, below the 75 a Yes needs. Hygiene is the only lens averaging under 2.5, so it stops short of a No. The product pages make a clear, well-proven case. The site loses visitors at the step after that: six CTA labels feed a thin contact page, proof numbers disagree from page to page, and test pages and duplicate URLs sit in the index.

To move to Yes, Path and Hygiene need to average 3.5 or better. One conversion pattern, one stat source and a clean index would get most of the way there.

One concrete fix

Route all six CTA labels to two intents, talk to sales and start a trial. Rebuild /get-started with a server-rendered form within 30 days, then watch demo-request completion from the product pages.

Method

How this was scored

Rubric

Message
Is it clear within one screen what this page offers and for whom?
Path
Is there one obvious, working next step that fits the visitor's intent?
Proof
Are claims backed by named, dated, sourced evidence?
Hygiene
Free of duplicates, placeholders, typos, broken links and stale content?
Findable
Reachable from navigation, one canonical URL, a proper H1 and metadata?

Evidence

Each page was read as served HTML, the same view a search crawler or text-only reader gets, along with the live navigation, footer and XML sitemap. Wireframes are drawn from that structure, not from screenshots.

Limits

Content that renders only in the browser (some forms, the careers listings, the leadership grid) may exist for human visitors even where flagged as missing here. Those findings are worded as "not in the served page". This review covers no traffic, speed or accessibility tooling data, and the scores reflect editorial judgement.
